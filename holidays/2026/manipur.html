In 2026, the list Ekam uses for Manipur has 63 holidays. It does not separate gazetted and restricted holidays — see the note under Source.

All 63 holidays in 2026

January 5

  1. Jan1

    New Year Day - Gaan -Ngai

    Thursday · 1 January

    New Year's Day, falling with Gaan-Ngai, the post-harvest festival of the Zeliangrong Nagas.

  2. Jan9

    Death Anniversary of (L) Maharaja Gambhir Singh

    Friday · 9 January

    Marks the death of Gambhir Singh, the Manipuri king who recovered the kingdom from Burmese occupation.

  3. Jan14

    Uttarayan -Makara Sankranti

    Wednesday · 14 January

    Marks the sun's passage into Capricorn and the start of its northward journey.

  4. Jan23

    Netajis Birthday- Saraswati Puja

    Friday · 23 January

    The birth of Subhas Chandra Bose, falling with the worship of Saraswati, goddess of learning.

  5. Jan26

    Republic Day

    Monday · 26 January

    Marks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.

February 5

  1. Feb4

    Sab-e-Barat

    Wednesday · 4 February

    The night of records, kept with prayer and remembrance of the dead in the month before Ramadan.

  2. Feb15

    Lui -Ngai-Ni

    Sunday · 15 February

    The seed-sowing festival of the Naga communities of Manipur, marking the start of the cultivation year.

  3. Feb16

    Shiva Ratri

    Monday · 16 February

    The great night of Shiva, kept with fasting and night-long worship.

  4. Feb20

    Zomi Namni

    Friday · 20 February

    The Zomi national day of Manipur, marking the community's identity and unity.

  5. Feb25

    Players Day

    Wednesday · 25 February

    The Manipuri day honouring the state's sportspeople.

March 10

  1. Mar3

    Yaosang (Doljatra)

    Tuesday · 3 March

    The Manipuri spring festival of five days, opening with the burning of a thatched hut and closing with the Thabal Chongba dance.

  2. Mar4

    Yaosang 2nd Day

    Wednesday · 4 March

    The second day of Manipur's Yaosang spring festival, when children collect nakatheng offerings.

  3. Mar6

    Yaosang 4th Day

    Friday · 6 March

    The closing day of Manipur's Yaosang spring festival.

  4. Mar8

    Bijoy Govinda Halankar

    Sunday · 8 March

    A Manipuri Vaishnav observance in the Yaosang festival season.

  5. Mar17

    Baruni -Sab-e- Qadr

    Tuesday · 17 March

    Baruni, the Manipuri bathing observance, falling with Shab-e-Qadr, the Night of Power in Ramadan.

  6. Mar19

    Sajibu Nongmapanba (Cheiraoba)

    Thursday · 19 March

    The Meitei new year of Manipur, on which families climb a hill to look towards the year ahead.

  7. Mar20

    Jamat - Ul -Vida

    Friday · 20 March

    The last Friday of Ramadan, drawing the largest congregational prayers of the year.

  8. Mar21

    Id- ul - Fitr

    Saturday · 21 March

    Marks the end of Ramadan's month of fasting, celebrated with congregational prayer and family meals.

  9. Mar25

    Kongba Leithong fatpa

    Wednesday · 25 March

    A Manipuri observance kept in the Yaosang festival season.

  10. Mar31

    Mahavir Jayanti

    Tuesday · 31 March

    Marks the birth of Mahavira, the twenty-fourth and last Tirthankara of Jainism.

April 7

  1. Apr1

    Yearly closing of Banks Account

    Wednesday · 1 April

    The annual closing of bank accounts on 1 April. Banks are shut; government offices stay open.

  2. Apr3

    Good Friday

    Friday · 3 April

    Commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, observed by Christians with fasting, prayer and church services.

  3. Apr7

    Hun- Thadou Cultural Festival

    Tuesday · 7 April

    The cultural festival of the Thadou community of Manipur.

  4. Apr11

    176th Death Anniversary of Maharaja Nara Singh

    Saturday · 11 April

    Marks the death of Maharaja Nara Singh, ruler of Manipur, remembered for repelling the Burmese invasion.

  5. Apr14

    Cheiraoba

    Tuesday · 14 April

    Sajibu Nongma Panba, the Meitei new year of Manipur, on which families climb a hill to look towards the year ahead.

  6. Apr15

    Shilhenba

    Wednesday · 15 April

    A Manipuri observance kept in the days following the Meitei new year.

  7. Apr23

    Khongjom Day

    Thursday · 23 April

    Honours the Manipuri soldiers who fell at the battle of Khongjom against the British in 1891.

May 2

  1. May1

    May Day (Labour Day) - Buddha Purnima

    Friday · 1 May

    International Workers' Day, falling with Buddha Purnima.

  2. May27

    Id- ul - Zuha (Bakrid)

    Wednesday · 27 May

    The festival of sacrifice, commemorating Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, marked by the sharing of meat.

June 1

  1. Jun26

    Muharram

    Friday · 26 June

    The first month of the Islamic year; the tenth day, Ashura, is kept in mourning for the martyrdom of Imam Husayn at Karbala.

July 3

  1. Jul16

    Kang (Rathajatra)

    Thursday · 16 July

    The Manipuri chariot festival of Jagannath, drawn through the streets over ten days.

  2. Jul24

    Kanglen (Punarjatra)

    Friday · 24 July

    The return chariot journey closing Manipur's Kang festival.

  3. Jul26

    Harisayan

    Sunday · 26 July

    Devshayani Ekadashi, on which Vishnu is held to begin his four months of sleep and the wedding season closes.

August 6

  1. Aug12

    Akheri Chahar Shumba

    Wednesday · 12 August

    The last Wednesday of Safar, marked by Muslims as the day the Prophet Muhammad last recovered from illness.

  2. Aug13

    Patriots Day

    Thursday · 13 August

    Honours the Manipuri who died in the Anglo-Manipur War of 1891.

  3. Aug15

    Independence Day

    Saturday · 15 August

    Marks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947, observed with flag hoisting and national ceremonies.

  4. Aug23

    Dhulon Houba

    Sunday · 23 August

    The start of the Manipuri Jhulan Yatra, in which Radha and Krishna are placed on a swing.

  5. Aug26

    Milad -un- Nabi (Id-e- Milad)

    Wednesday · 26 August

    Marks the birth of the Prophet Muhammad, observed with processions, readings and charity.

  6. Aug28

    Jhulon Loiba

    Friday · 28 August

    The closing of the Manipuri Jhulan Yatra swing festival.

September 5

  1. Sep4

    Janma Ashtami

    Friday · 4 September

    Marks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.

  2. Sep19

    Radha Ashtami

    Saturday · 19 September

    Marks the birth of Radha, the consort of Krishna, kept a fortnight after Janmashtami.

  3. Sep22

    Heikru Hidongba

    Tuesday · 22 September

    The Manipuri boat race on the Bijoy Govinda moat, rowed in honour of the deity Bijoy Govinda.

  4. Sep27

    Tarpon Houba

    Sunday · 27 September

    The opening of the Manipuri tarpan, the offering of water to ancestors.

  5. Sep30

    Jananeta Irawat Birth Day

    Wednesday · 30 September

    Marks the birth of Hijam Irawat, the Manipuri leader who campaigned against feudal privilege and colonial rule.

October 8

  1. Oct2

    Gandhi Jayanti

    Friday · 2 October

    Birth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement and advocate of non-violent resistance.

  2. Oct10

    Tarpon Loiba

    Saturday · 10 October

    The closing of the Manipuri tarpan, the offering of water to ancestors.

  3. Oct11

    Mera Chaoren Houba

    Sunday · 11 October

    The opening of Manipur's Mera Chaoren, kept in the month of Mera.

  4. Oct17

    Kuki Rising Day

    Saturday · 17 October

    Marks the Kuki rising of 1917-19 against British recruitment for the First World War.

  5. Oct19

    Durga Ashtami (Bor Numit)

    Monday · 19 October

    The eighth day of Durga Puja, called Bor Numit, the great day, in Manipur.

  6. Oct21

    Kwak Jatra (Dussehra)

    Wednesday · 21 October

    The Manipuri Dussehra observance, closing with the ceremonial release of a crow.

  7. Oct26

    Mera Houchongba

    Monday · 26 October

    The Manipuri festival renewing the bond between the hill and valley communities.

  8. Oct30

    Chaga Ngee

    Friday · 30 October

    The Zeliangrong Naga festival of Manipur, kept for the well-being of the village.

November 6

  1. Nov1

    Kut

    Sunday · 1 November

    The post-harvest thanksgiving of the Kuki-Chin-Mizo communities of Manipur, celebrated with dance and feasting.

  2. Nov9

    Diwali (Dipavali)

    Monday · 9 November

    The festival of lights, marking the triumph of light over darkness and Rama's return to Ayodhya.

  3. Nov10

    Govardhon Puja

    Tuesday · 10 November

    Marks Krishna lifting Mount Govardhan to shelter the villagers from Indra's storm, kept the day after Diwali.

  4. Nov11

    Ningol Chakkouba

    Wednesday · 11 November

    The Manipuri festival at which married daughters return to their parents' home for a feast.

  5. Nov18

    Gosta Ashtami

    Wednesday · 18 November

    A Manipuri Vaishnav observance marking Krishna's first taking of the cows to pasture.

  6. Nov24

    Guru Nanaks Jayanti- Mera Waphukpa

    Tuesday · 24 November

    The birth of Guru Nanak, falling with Mera Waphukpa, the Manipuri festival renewing the bond between hill and valley peoples.

December 5

  1. Dec12

    Nupi -Lal

    Saturday · 12 December

    Honours the women of Manipur who rose against colonial rule and grain export in 1939.

  2. Dec24

    Christmas Eve

    Thursday · 24 December

    The evening before Christmas Day, marked with midnight services and family celebrations.

  3. Dec25

    Christmas

    Friday · 25 December

    Celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.

  4. Dec26

    Post Christmas

    Saturday · 26 December

    One of the days Manipur sets aside after Christmas for continued celebration.

  5. Dec31

    New Years Eve

    Thursday · 31 December

    The last day of the Gregorian year.
