Goa Holiday List 2026

Gazetted and restricted holidays, with dates and weekdays

In 2026, Goa has 18 gazetted holidays and 24 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

See these holidays on a calendar, plan your leave around them and get reminders — with Ekam, a free Android app.

Gazetted holidays (18)

DateDayHoliday
26 JanuaryMondayRepublic DayMarks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.
3 MarchTuesdayHoli (Dhulivandana)The day of colours following the Holika bonfire, called Dhulivandan in the Konkan.
19 MarchThursdayGudi PadavaThe Marathi new year, on which a decorated gudi is raised outside the house for prosperity.
21 MarchSaturdayId-Ul-Fitr (Ramzan Id)Marks the end of Ramadan's month of fasting, celebrated with congregational prayer and family meals.
26 MarchThursdayRam NavamiMarks the birth of Rama, the seventh avatar of Vishnu, celebrated with readings of the Ramayana.
3 AprilFridayGood FridayCommemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, observed by Christians with fasting, prayer and church services.
14 AprilTuesdayBirth Anniversary of Dr. Babasaheb AmbedkarMarks the birth of B. R. Ambedkar, principal architect of the Constitution of India and campaigner against caste discrimination.
1 MayFridayMay DayInternational Workers' Day, marking the labour movement's campaign for the eight-hour working day.
28 MayThursdayId-Ul-Zuha (Bakri Id)The festival of sacrifice, commemorating Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, marked by the sharing of meat.
15 AugustSaturdayIndependence DayMarks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947, observed with flag hoisting and national ceremonies.
14 SeptemberMondayHartalika / Ganesh Chaturthi (1st Day)Hartalika Teej, the fast kept in honour of Parvati's union with Shiva, opening Goa's Ganesh festival.
15 SeptemberTuesdayGanesh Chaturthi (2nd Day)The second day of the Ganesh festival, kept in Goa as the celebration continues.
2 OctoberFridayGandhi JayantiBirth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement and advocate of non-violent resistance.
20 OctoberTuesdayDussehra (Vijaya Dashmi)The tenth day, marking Rama's victory over Ravana and Durga's over Mahishasura.
8 NovemberSundayDiwali (Deepavali)The festival of lights, marking the triumph of light over darkness and Rama's return to Ayodhya.
3 DecemberThursdayFeast of St. Francis XavierHonours Francis Xavier, the missionary whose remains lie at the Basilica of Bom Jesus in Goa.
19 DecemberSaturdayGoa Liberation DayMarks the end of Portuguese rule in Goa in 1961 and its accession to India.
25 DecemberFridayChristmas DayCelebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.

Restricted holidays (24)

DateDayHoliday
1 JanuaryThursdayNew Year DayMarks the first day of the year on the Gregorian calendar, celebrated globally with festivities and new resolutions.
14 JanuaryWednesdayMakarsankrantiMarks the sun's passage into Capricorn and the start of longer days.
16 JanuaryFridayFeast of St. Joseph VazHonours Joseph Vaz, the Goan priest who ministered in Sri Lanka under Dutch persecution.
1 FebruarySundayGuru Ravi Das BirthdayMarks the birth of Ravidas, the fifteenth-century poet-saint of the Bhakti movement.
15 FebruarySundayMahashivratriThe great night of Shiva, kept with fasting and night-long worship.
19 FebruaryThursdayShivaji JayantiMarks the birth of Shivaji, founder of the Maratha empire.
31 MarchTuesdayMahavir JayantiMarks the birth of Mahavira, the twenty-fourth and last Tirthankara of Jainism.
2 AprilThursdayMaundy ThursdayCommemorates the Last Supper on the Thursday before Easter.
14 AprilTuesdayVaisakhi / VishuThe spring harvest new year, kept as Vaisakhi in Punjab and Vishu in Kerala.
1 MayFridayBuddha PurnimaMarks the birth, enlightenment and passing of Gautama Buddha, all traditionally held to have fallen on the same full-moon day.
12 JuneFridayFeast of Sacred Heart of JesusThe Catholic devotion to the heart of Jesus as the emblem of divine love.
26 JuneFridayMuharramThe first month of the Islamic year; the tenth day, Ashura, is kept in mourning for the martyrdom of Imam Husayn at Karbala.
25 AugustTuesdayMilad-Un-Nabi or Id-e-MiladMarks the birth of the Prophet Muhammad, observed with processions, readings and charity.
26 AugustWednesdayOnamThe harvest festival of Kerala, marking the annual homecoming of the mythical king Mahabali.
28 AugustFridayRaksha BandhanSisters tie a protective thread on their brothers' wrists in exchange for a promise of protection.
4 SeptemberFridayJanmashtamiMarks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.
2 NovemberMondayAll Souls DayThe Christian day of prayer for the dead, kept the day after All Saints' Day.
10 NovemberTuesdayGovardhan PujaMarks Krishna lifting Mount Govardhan to shelter the villagers from Indra's storm, kept the day after Diwali.
11 NovemberWednesdayBhaubijSisters mark their brothers' foreheads and pray for their long life, two days after Diwali.
24 NovemberTuesdayGuru Nanak's BirthdayMarks the birth of Guru Nanak, the founder of Sikhism, observed with processions and scripture readings.
24 NovemberTuesdayGuru Teg Bahadur Martyrdom DayMarks the execution of the ninth Sikh Guru in 1675 for defending the right to religious freedom.
8 DecemberTuesdayFeast of Immaculate Conception of MaryThe Catholic feast holding that Mary was conceived free of original sin.
24 DecemberThursdayChristmas EveThe evening before Christmas Day, marked with midnight services and family celebrations.
31 DecemberThursdayNew Year's EveThe last day of the Gregorian year.

Gazetted holidays on a Monday or Friday

These sit next to a weekend — a long weekend for anyone who has the Saturday off.

  • Monday, 26 January — Republic Day
  • Friday, 3 April — Good Friday
  • Friday, 1 May — May Day
  • Monday, 14 September — Hartalika / Ganesh Chaturthi (1st Day)
  • Friday, 2 October — Gandhi Jayanti
  • Friday, 25 December — Christmas Day
