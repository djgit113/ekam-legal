In 2026, Goa has 18 gazetted holidays and 24 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

All 42 holidays in 2026

January 4

  1. Jan1

    New Year Day

    Restricted

    Thursday · 1 January

    Marks the first day of the year on the Gregorian calendar, celebrated globally with festivities and new resolutions.

  2. Jan14

    Makarsankranti

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 14 January

    Marks the sun's passage into Capricorn and the start of longer days.

  3. Jan16

    Feast of St. Joseph Vaz

    Restricted

    Friday · 16 January

    Honours Joseph Vaz, the Goan priest who ministered in Sri Lanka under Dutch persecution.

  4. Jan26

    Republic Day

    Gazetted

    Monday · 26 January

    Marks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.

February 3

  1. Feb1

    Guru Ravi Das Birthday

    Restricted

    Sunday · 1 February

    Marks the birth of Ravidas, the fifteenth-century poet-saint of the Bhakti movement.

  2. Feb15

    Mahashivratri

    Restricted

    Sunday · 15 February

    The great night of Shiva, kept with fasting and night-long worship.

  3. Feb19

    Shivaji Jayanti

    Restricted

    Thursday · 19 February

    Marks the birth of Shivaji, founder of the Maratha empire.

March 5

  1. Mar3

    Holi (Dhulivandana)

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 3 March

    The day of colours following the Holika bonfire, called Dhulivandan in the Konkan.

  2. Mar19

    Gudi Padava

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 19 March

    The Marathi new year, on which a decorated gudi is raised outside the house for prosperity.

  3. Mar21

    Id-Ul-Fitr (Ramzan Id)

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 21 March

    Marks the end of Ramadan's month of fasting, celebrated with congregational prayer and family meals.

  4. Mar26

    Ram Navami

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 26 March

    Marks the birth of Rama, the seventh avatar of Vishnu, celebrated with readings of the Ramayana.

  5. Mar31

    Mahavir Jayanti

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 31 March

    Marks the birth of Mahavira, the twenty-fourth and last Tirthankara of Jainism.

April 4

  1. Apr2

    Maundy Thursday

    Restricted

    Thursday · 2 April

    Commemorates the Last Supper on the Thursday before Easter.

  2. Apr3

    Good Friday

    Gazetted

    Friday · 3 April

    Commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, observed by Christians with fasting, prayer and church services.

  3. Apr14

    Birth Anniversary of Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 14 April

    Marks the birth of B. R. Ambedkar, principal architect of the Constitution of India and campaigner against caste discrimination.

  4. Apr14

    Vaisakhi / Vishu

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 14 April

    The spring harvest new year, kept as Vaisakhi in Punjab and Vishu in Kerala.

May 3

  1. May1

    Buddha Purnima

    Restricted

    Friday · 1 May

    Marks the birth, enlightenment and passing of Gautama Buddha, all traditionally held to have fallen on the same full-moon day.

  2. May1

    May Day

    Gazetted

    Friday · 1 May

    International Workers' Day, marking the labour movement's campaign for the eight-hour working day.

  3. May28

    Id-Ul-Zuha (Bakri Id)

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 28 May

    The festival of sacrifice, commemorating Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, marked by the sharing of meat.

June 2

  1. Jun12

    Feast of Sacred Heart of Jesus

    Restricted

    Friday · 12 June

    The Catholic devotion to the heart of Jesus as the emblem of divine love.

  2. Jun26

    Muharram

    Restricted

    Friday · 26 June

    The first month of the Islamic year; the tenth day, Ashura, is kept in mourning for the martyrdom of Imam Husayn at Karbala.

August 4

  1. Aug15

    Independence Day

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 15 August

    Marks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947, observed with flag hoisting and national ceremonies.

  2. Aug25

    Milad-Un-Nabi or Id-e-Milad

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 25 August

    Marks the birth of the Prophet Muhammad, observed with processions, readings and charity.

  3. Aug26

    Onam

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 26 August

    The harvest festival of Kerala, marking the annual homecoming of the mythical king Mahabali.

  4. Aug28

    Raksha Bandhan

    Restricted

    Friday · 28 August

    Sisters tie a protective thread on their brothers' wrists in exchange for a promise of protection.

September 3

  1. Sep4

    Janmashtami

    Restricted

    Friday · 4 September

    Marks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.

  2. Sep14

    Hartalika / Ganesh Chaturthi (1st Day)

    Gazetted

    Monday · 14 September

    Hartalika Teej, the fast kept in honour of Parvati's union with Shiva, opening Goa's Ganesh festival.

  3. Sep15

    Ganesh Chaturthi (2nd Day)

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 15 September

    The second day of the Ganesh festival, kept in Goa as the celebration continues.

October 2

  1. Oct2

    Gandhi Jayanti

    Gazetted

    Friday · 2 October

    Birth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement and advocate of non-violent resistance.

  2. Oct20

    Dussehra (Vijaya Dashmi)

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 20 October

    The tenth day, marking Rama's victory over Ravana and Durga's over Mahishasura.

November 6

  1. Nov2

    All Souls Day

    Restricted

    Monday · 2 November

    The Christian day of prayer for the dead, kept the day after All Saints' Day.

  2. Nov8

    Diwali (Deepavali)

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 8 November

    The festival of lights, marking the triumph of light over darkness and Rama's return to Ayodhya.

  3. Nov10

    Govardhan Puja

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 10 November

    Marks Krishna lifting Mount Govardhan to shelter the villagers from Indra's storm, kept the day after Diwali.

  4. Nov11

    Bhaubij

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 11 November

    Sisters mark their brothers' foreheads and pray for their long life, two days after Diwali.

  5. Nov24

    Guru Nanak's Birthday

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 24 November

    Marks the birth of Guru Nanak, the founder of Sikhism, observed with processions and scripture readings.

  6. Nov24

    Guru Teg Bahadur Martyrdom Day

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 24 November

    Marks the execution of the ninth Sikh Guru in 1675 for defending the right to religious freedom.

December 6

  1. Dec3

    Feast of St. Francis Xavier

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 3 December

    Honours Francis Xavier, the missionary whose remains lie at the Basilica of Bom Jesus in Goa.

  2. Dec8

    Feast of Immaculate Conception of Mary

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 8 December

    The Catholic feast holding that Mary was conceived free of original sin.

  3. Dec19

    Goa Liberation Day

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 19 December

    Marks the end of Portuguese rule in Goa in 1961 and its accession to India.

  4. Dec24

    Christmas Eve

    Restricted

    Thursday · 24 December

    The evening before Christmas Day, marked with midnight services and family celebrations.

  5. Dec25

    Christmas Day

    Gazetted

    Friday · 25 December

    Celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.

  6. Dec31

    New Year's Eve

    Restricted

    Thursday · 31 December

    The last day of the Gregorian year.
