West Bengal Holiday List 2026

Gazetted and restricted holidays, with dates and weekdays

In 2026, West Bengal has 55 gazetted holidays and 8 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.
