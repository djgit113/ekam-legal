West Bengal Holiday List 2026

In 2026, West Bengal has 55 gazetted holidays and 8 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

All 63 holidays in 2026

January 6

  1. Jan1

    New Year's Day

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 1 January

    Marks the first day of the year on the Gregorian calendar, celebrated globally with festivities and new resolutions.

  2. Jan12

    Birthday of Swami Vivekananda

    Gazetted

    Monday · 12 January

    Marks the birth of Swami Vivekananda, who introduced Vedanta to the West and founded the Ramakrishna Mission.

  3. Jan22

    Day before Saraswati Puja

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 22 January

    The preparation day before the worship of Saraswati, kept as a state holiday in West Bengal.

  4. Jan23

    Netaji's Birthday

    Gazetted

    Friday · 23 January

    Celebrates the birth anniversary of Subhas Chandra Bose, a prominent and influential leader in the Indian independence movement.

  5. Jan23

    Saraswati Puja (Sree Panchami)

    Gazetted

    Friday · 23 January

    Worship of Saraswati, goddess of learning, on which children are often given their first lessons in writing.

  6. Jan26

    Republic Day

    Gazetted

    Monday · 26 January

    Marks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.

February 5

  1. Feb1

    Birthday of Bir Chilarai

    Restricted

    Sunday · 1 February

    Honours Chilarai, the Koch general remembered for the speed of his campaigns, revered across North Bengal and Assam.

  2. Feb1

    Birthday of Guru Rabidas

    Restricted

    Sunday · 1 February

    Marks the birth of Ravidas, the fifteenth-century poet-saint of the Bhakti movement.

  3. Feb4

    Shab-e-Barat

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 4 February

    The night of records, kept with prayer and remembrance of the dead in the month before Ramadan.

  4. Feb14

    Birthday of Thakur Panchanan Barma

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 14 February

    Marks the birth of Panchanan Barma, the social reformer and leader of the Rajbanshi community.

  5. Feb15

    Shivaratri

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 15 February

    The great night of Shiva, kept with fasting and night-long worship.

March 7

  1. Mar3

    Doljatra

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 3 March

    The Bengali form of Holi, in which idols of Radha and Krishna are carried in procession and coloured powder is offered.

  2. Mar4

    Holi (Day after Doljatra)

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 4 March

    The second day of the spring colour festival in West Bengal, following Doljatra.

  3. Mar17

    Birthday of Shri Shri Harichand Thakur

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 17 March

    Marks the birth of Harichand Thakur, founder of the Matua religious movement in Bengal.

  4. Mar20

    Day before Eid-Ul-Fitr

    Gazetted

    Friday · 20 March

    The eve of Eid-ul-Fitr, kept as a state holiday in West Bengal for preparations.

  5. Mar21

    Eid-Ul-Fitr

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 21 March

    Marks the end of Ramadan's month of fasting, celebrated with congregational prayer, charity and family meals.

  6. Mar26

    Ram Navami

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 26 March

    Marks the birth of Rama, the seventh avatar of Vishnu, celebrated with readings of the Ramayana.

  7. Mar31

    Mahavir Jayanti

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 31 March

    Marks the birth of Mahavira, the twenty-fourth and last Tirthankara of Jainism.

April 5

  1. Apr1

    Yearly Closing of Bank Account

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 1 April

    The annual closing of bank accounts on 1 April. Banks are shut; government offices and treasuries stay open.

  2. Apr3

    Good Friday

    Gazetted

    Friday · 3 April

    Commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, observed by Christians with fasting, prayer and church services.

  3. Apr4

    Easter Saturday

    Restricted

    Saturday · 4 April

    The day between Good Friday and Easter Sunday, kept by many Christian communities as a day of vigil.

  4. Apr14

    Birthday of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 14 April

    Marks the birth of B. R. Ambedkar, principal architect of the Constitution of India and campaigner against caste discrimination.

  5. Apr15

    Bengali New Year's Day (Nababarsha)

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 15 April

    Poila Boishakh, the first day of the Bengali calendar year, marked with new account books, processions and family meals.

May 6

  1. May1

    Birthday of Pandit Raghunath Murmu

    Restricted

    Friday · 1 May

    Marks the birth of Raghunath Murmu, creator of the Ol Chiki script for the Santali language.

  2. May1

    Buddha Purnima

    Gazetted

    Friday · 1 May

    Marks the birth, enlightenment and passing of Gautama Buddha, all traditionally held to have fallen on the same full-moon day.

  3. May1

    May Day

    Gazetted

    Friday · 1 May

    International Workers' Day, marking the labour movement's campaign for the eight-hour working day.

  4. May9

    Birthday of Rabindranath Tagore

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 9 May

    Marks the birth of Rabindranath Tagore, poet and composer, the first non-European to win the Nobel Prize in Literature.

  5. May26

    Day before Id-Ud-Zoha (Bakrid)

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 26 May

    The eve of Bakrid, kept as a state holiday in West Bengal for preparations.

  6. May27

    Id-Ud-Zoha (Bakrid)

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 27 May

    The festival of sacrifice, commemorating Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, marked by the sharing of meat.

June 2

  1. Jun26

    Muharram

    Gazetted

    Friday · 26 June

    The first month of the Islamic year; the tenth day, Ashura, is kept in mourning for the martyrdom of Imam Husayn at Karbala.

  2. Jun30

    Hul Diwas

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 30 June

    Marks the Santhal rebellion of 1855 against colonial and landlord oppression.

July 2

  1. Jul13

    Birthday of Poet Bhanu Bhakt

    Restricted

    Monday · 13 July

    Marks the birth of Bhanubhakta Acharya, the poet who first translated the Ramayana into Nepali.

  2. Jul16

    Rathayatra

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 16 July

    The chariot festival of Jagannath, in which the deities are drawn through the streets in great wooden chariots.

August 3

  1. Aug15

    Independence Day

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 15 August

    Marks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947, observed with flag hoisting and national ceremonies.

  2. Aug26

    Fateha-Dwaz-Daham

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 26 August

    The death anniversary of the Prophet Muhammad, observed with readings and offerings.

  3. Aug28

    Rakhi Bandhan

    Gazetted

    Friday · 28 August

    Sisters tie a protective thread on their brothers' wrists in exchange for a promise of protection.

September 2

  1. Sep4

    Janmastami

    Gazetted

    Friday · 4 September

    Marks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.

  2. Sep17

    Viswakarma Puja

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 17 September

    Worship of Vishwakarma, the divine architect, on which tools and machinery are honoured in workshops and factories.

October 14

  1. Oct2

    Birthday of Gandhiji

    Gazetted

    Friday · 2 October

    Birth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement and advocate of non-violent resistance.

  2. Oct10

    Mahalaya

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 10 October

    Marks the end of Pitru Paksha and the start of Devi Paksha, when the goddess Durga is invited to descend.

  3. Oct15

    Durga Puja, Maha Chaturthi

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 15 October

    The fourth day of Durga Puja, when preparations and pandal visits begin in earnest.

  4. Oct16

    Durga Puja, Maha Panchami

    Gazetted

    Friday · 16 October

    The fifth day of Durga Puja, on which the goddess is welcomed and pandals open to the public.

  5. Oct17

    Durga Puja, Maha Shashthi

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 17 October

    The sixth day, on which the goddess's face is unveiled and the festival formally begins.

  6. Oct18

    Durga Puja, Maha Saptami

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 18 October

    The seventh day, marked by the Nabapatrika bathing ritual at dawn.

  7. Oct19

    Durga Puja, Maha Astami

    Gazetted

    Monday · 19 October

    The eighth and most sacred day of Durga Puja, marked by Anjali offerings and the Sandhi Puja.

  8. Oct20

    Durga Puja, Maha Nabami

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 20 October

    The ninth day, on which the final major offerings are made before the goddess departs.

  9. Oct21

    Durga Puja, Dasami

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 21 October

    Vijaya Dashami, the tenth day, on which the idols are immersed and the goddess is bidden farewell.

  10. Oct22

    Additional Day for Durga Puja

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 22 October

    An extra state holiday granted around Durga Puja, when public life in West Bengal is largely suspended.

  11. Oct23

    Additional Day for Durga Puja

    Gazetted

    Friday · 23 October

    An extra state holiday granted around Durga Puja, when public life in West Bengal is largely suspended.

  12. Oct24

    Additional Day for Durga Puja

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 24 October

    An extra state holiday granted around Durga Puja, when public life in West Bengal is largely suspended.

  13. Oct25

    Lakshmi Puja

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 25 October

    Worship of Lakshmi, goddess of wealth and prosperity, on the full moon following Durga Puja in Bengal.

  14. Oct26

    Additional Day for Lakshmi Puja

    Gazetted

    Monday · 26 October

    An extra state holiday granted around Lakshmi Puja in West Bengal.
