Assam Holiday List 2026

Gazetted and restricted holidays, with dates and weekdays

In 2026, Assam has 33 gazetted holidays and 37 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.
