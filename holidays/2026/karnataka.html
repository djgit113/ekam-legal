In 2026, Karnataka has 25 gazetted holidays and 27 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

All 52 holidays in 2026

January 4

  1. Jan1

    Nootana Varsharambha (New Year's Day)

    Restricted

    Thursday · 1 January

    The first day of the year on the Gregorian calendar.

  2. Jan15

    Uttarayana Punyakala, Makara Sankranti

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 15 January

    Marks the sun's passage into Capricorn and the start of its northward journey, held to be an auspicious time.

  3. Jan26

    Ganarajyotsava (Republic Day)

    Gazetted

    Monday · 26 January

    Marks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force.

  4. Jan27

    Madhva Navami

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 27 January

    Marks the day the philosopher Madhvacharya, founder of the Dvaita school, is held to have departed.

February 2

  1. Feb4

    Shab-e-Barat

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 4 February

    The night of records, kept with prayer and remembrance of the dead in the month before Ramadan.

  2. Feb15

    Maha Shivaratri

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 15 February

    The great night of Shiva, kept with fasting and night-long worship.

March 8

  1. Mar2

    Holi Habba

    Restricted

    Monday · 2 March

    The festival of colours, marking the arrival of spring and the triumph of good over evil.

  2. Mar17

    Shab-e-Qadar

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 17 March

    The Night of Power in the last ten days of Ramadan, held to be the night the Quran was first revealed.

  3. Mar19

    Yugadi Habba

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 19 March

    The new year of the Deccan, marked with a preparation combining six tastes to represent the year's mixture of experiences.

  4. Mar20

    Jumat-ul-Vida

    Restricted

    Friday · 20 March

    The last Friday of Ramadan, drawing the largest congregational prayers of the year.

  5. Mar21

    Khutub-e-Ramzan (Id-ul-Fitr)

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 21 March

    Marks the end of Ramadan's month of fasting, celebrated with congregational prayer and family meals.

  6. Mar23

    Devara Dasimayya Jayanti

    Restricted

    Monday · 23 March

    Marks the birth of Devara Dasimayya, the tenth-century Kannada poet-saint of the Veerashaiva tradition.

  7. Mar27

    Sri Rama Navami

    Restricted

    Friday · 27 March

    Marks the birth of Rama, the seventh avatar of Vishnu, celebrated with readings of the Ramayana.

  8. Mar31

    Mahaveera Jayanti

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 31 March

    Marks the birth of Mahavira, the twenty-fourth and last Tirthankara of Jainism.

April 7

  1. Apr3

    Good Friday

    Gazetted

    Friday · 3 April

    Commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, observed by Christians with fasting, prayer and church services.

  2. Apr4

    Holy Saturday

    Restricted

    Saturday · 4 April

    The day between Good Friday and Easter Sunday, kept by Christians as a day of vigil.

  3. Apr14

    Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Jayanti

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 14 April

    Marks the birth of B. R. Ambedkar, principal architect of the Constitution of India.

  4. Apr14

    Sauramana Yugadi

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 14 April

    The solar new year of the Deccan, kept a few weeks after the lunar Yugadi.

  5. Apr20

    Basava Jayanti, Akshaya Tritiya

    Gazetted

    Monday · 20 April

    Marks the birth of Basavanna, founder of the Lingayat tradition, falling with Akshaya Tritiya.

  6. Apr21

    Sri Shankaracharya Jayanti

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 21 April

    Marks the birth of Adi Shankaracharya, the philosopher who consolidated Advaita Vedanta.

  7. Apr22

    Sri Ramanujacharya Jayanti

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 22 April

    Marks the birth of Ramanujacharya, the philosopher of Vishishtadvaita who opened temple worship to all castes.

May 3

  1. May1

    Buddha Purnima

    Restricted

    Friday · 1 May

    Marks the birth, enlightenment and passing of Gautama Buddha, all traditionally held to have fallen on the same full-moon day.

  2. May1

    Karmika Dinacharane (May Day)

    Gazetted

    Friday · 1 May

    International Workers' Day, marking the labour movement's campaign for the eight-hour working day.

  3. May28

    Bakrid

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 28 May

    The festival of sacrifice, commemorating Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, marked by the sharing of meat.

June 1

  1. Jun26

    Moharam Kade Dina

    Gazetted

    Friday · 26 June

    The last day of the Muharram observance, closing the mourning for Imam Husayn.

August 6

  1. Aug15

    Swatantrya Dinacharane (Independence Day)

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 15 August

    Marks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947.

  2. Aug21

    Sri Varamahalakshmi Vrata

    Restricted

    Friday · 21 August

    A Friday fast kept by married women in southern India for the family's prosperity and well-being.

  3. Aug26

    Eid-Milad

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 26 August

    Marks the birth of the Prophet Muhammad, observed with processions, readings and charity.

  4. Aug26

    Rig Upakarma / Tiruvonam

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 26 August

    The day Rigvedic Brahmins renew the sacred thread, falling with Tiruvonam, the chief day of Onam.

  5. Aug27

    Yajur Upakarma

    Restricted

    Thursday · 27 August

    The day Yajurvedic Brahmins renew the sacred thread, kept on the Shravana full moon.

  6. Aug28

    Brahmasri Narayanaguru Jayanti, Raksha Bandhana

    Restricted

    Friday · 28 August

    Marks the birth of Narayana Guru, the Kerala reformer against caste, falling with Raksha Bandhan.

September 7

  1. Sep3

    Kail Muhurta (Kodagu)

    Restricted

    Thursday · 3 September

    The Kodava festival at which weapons are cleaned and honoured once the paddy transplanting is done.

  2. Sep4

    Sri Krishna Janmashtami

    Restricted

    Friday · 4 September

    Marks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.

  3. Sep8

    Kanya Mariyamma Jayanti

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 8 September

    The Karnataka observance of the Nativity of Mary, kept by Catholic communities.

  4. Sep14

    Swarna Gowri Vrata

    Restricted

    Monday · 14 September

    The Karnataka fast kept by women in honour of Gauri, on the day before Ganesh Chaturthi.

  5. Sep14

    Varasiddhi Vinayaka Vrata

    Gazetted

    Monday · 14 September

    The Karnataka observance of Ganesh Chaturthi, the worship of Ganesha as granter of wishes.

  6. Sep17

    Vishwakarma Jayanti

    Restricted

    Thursday · 17 September

    Worship of Vishwakarma, the divine architect, on which tools and machinery are honoured.

  7. Sep25

    Sri Anantapadmanabha Vrata

    Restricted

    Friday · 25 September

    The worship of Vishnu as Ananta Padmanabha on the closing day of the Ganesh festival.

October 6

  1. Oct2

    Gandhi Jayanti

    Gazetted

    Friday · 2 October

    Birth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement and advocate of non-violent resistance.

  2. Oct10

    Mahalaya Amavasye

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 10 October

    The new-moon day closing the fortnight of ancestral offerings and opening the Durga Puja season.

  3. Oct18

    Tula Sankramana (Kodagu)

    Restricted

    Sunday · 18 October

    The Kodava observance of the sun's entry into Libra, kept with a bathing pilgrimage to Talakaveri.

  4. Oct20

    Mahanavami, Ayudha Puje

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 20 October

    The ninth day of Navratri, on which tools, books and machinery are worshipped.

  5. Oct21

    Vijayadashami

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 21 October

    The tenth day, marking the victory of good over evil and the close of the Navratri festival.

  6. Oct25

    Maharshi Valmiki Jayanti

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 25 October

    Marks the birth of Valmiki, author of the Ramayana.

November 6

  1. Nov1

    Kannada Rajyotsava

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 1 November

    Marks the formation of the state of Karnataka in 1956, unifying the Kannada-speaking regions.

  2. Nov8

    Naraka Chaturdashi

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 8 November

    Chhoti Diwali, marking Krishna's defeat of the demon Narakasura on the day before Diwali.

  3. Nov10

    Balipadyami, Deepavali

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 10 November

    The Diwali day on which King Bali's annual return is welcomed, and cattle and account books are honoured.

  4. Nov24

    Guru Nanak Jayanti

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 24 November

    Marks the birth of Guru Nanak, the founder of Sikhism, observed with processions and continuous reading of the Guru Granth Sahib.

  5. Nov26

    Huttari Habba

    Restricted

    Thursday · 26 November

    The harvest thanksgiving of Kodagu, at which the first sheaf of paddy is cut and carried home.

  6. Nov27

    Kanakadasa Jayanti

    Gazetted

    Friday · 27 November

    Marks the birth of Kanakadasa, the Kannada poet-saint who wrote against caste exclusion.

December 2

  1. Dec24

    Christmas Eve

    Restricted

    Thursday · 24 December

    The evening before Christmas Day, marked with midnight services and family celebrations.

  2. Dec25

    Christmas

    Gazetted

    Friday · 25 December

    Celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.
