Arunachal Pradesh Holiday List 2026

Every gazetted and restricted holiday in Arunachal Pradesh for 2026, from the state's own published list — with dates, weekdays and the long weekends.

In 2026, Arunachal Pradesh has 17 gazetted holidays and 68 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

All 85 holidays in 2026

January 9

  1. Jan1

    New Year's Day

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 1 January

    Marks the first day of the year on the Gregorian calendar, celebrated globally with festivities and new resolutions.

  2. Jan3

    Hazarat Ali's Birthday

    Restricted

    Saturday · 3 January

    Marks the birth of Ali ibn Abi Talib, cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet Muhammad.

  3. Jan6

    Si Donyi Hilo (Tagin)

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 6 January

    The Tagin community's festival honouring Si, the earth, and Donyi, the sun, with prayers for the well-being of the people.

  4. Jan9

    Torgya (Monpa)

    Restricted

    Friday · 9 January

    The Monpa monastic festival at Tawang, in which masked dances are performed to drive away evil and misfortune for the coming year.

  5. Jan11

    Sarok (Aka)

    Restricted

    Sunday · 11 January

    A festival of the Aka (Hrusso) community of Arunachal Pradesh, observed as a local holiday in their districts.

  6. Jan14

    Makar Sankranti

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 14 January

    Marks the sun's passage into Capricorn and the start of longer days, celebrated with kite flying, bonfires and sesame sweets.

  7. Jan14

    Pongal / Magha Bihu

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 14 January

    The Tamil harvest thanksgiving, coinciding with Assam's Magh Bihu harvest festival.

  8. Jan23

    Basant Panchami / Sri Panchami

    Restricted

    Friday · 23 January

    Marks the arrival of spring and the worship of Saraswati, goddess of learning.

  9. Jan26

    Republic Day

    Gazetted

    Monday · 26 January

    Marks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.

  12. Feb19

  15. Feb26

  8. Mar31
