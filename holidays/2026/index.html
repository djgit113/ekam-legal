More in Ekam

Ekam is more than a holiday list. Download it and you also get:

  • Yearly Calendar and leave plannerAll twelve months on one page. Plan your leave around the holidays and see which days make a long weekend.
  • RemindersReminders that ring at the exact minute, repeat when you want and survive a restart — plus a monthly reminder of the holidays ahead.
  • EventsA countdown, a shopping list, a checklist and what it is costing you — split between friends when the bill is shared.
  • Private diaryWrite about a day, add photographs, handwrite with a stylus, keep checklists and sort it all into folders.
  • Money ledgerMoney in and out, month by month, and who owes whom — with repayments and interest.
  • Activity trackerTrack anything you do daily, see your streaks, and what it earns or costs you.
  • Home-screen widgetsToday's date and holiday, or a clock with the date, in any size and in a style that suits your wallpaper.
  • Assamese calendarFor Assam: read the calendar in Assamese, with the Assamese date and holiday names.
  • Your data stays on your phoneNo account and no sign-in. Back up to a file you keep, and lock the app with your fingerprint or screen lock.
