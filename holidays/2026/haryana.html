Haryana Holiday List 2026

Gazetted and restricted holidays, with dates and weekdays

In 2026, Haryana has 28 gazetted holidays and 14 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

Gazetted holidays (28)

DateDayHoliday
23 JanuaryFridaySir Chottu Ram Jayanti / Basant PanchmiMarks the birth of Chhotu Ram, the Haryana leader who campaigned for the farming classes, falling with Basant Panchami.
26 JanuaryMondayRepublic DayMarks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.
1 FebruarySundayGuru Ravidas JayantiMarks the birth of Ravidas, the fifteenth-century poet-saint of the Bhakti movement.
15 FebruarySundayMaha ShivratriThe great night of Shiva, kept with fasting and night-long worship.
4 MarchWednesdayHoliThe festival of colours, marking the arrival of spring and the triumph of good over evil.
21 MarchSaturdayId-ul-FitrThe Islamic festival marking the end of Ramadan, celebrated with communal prayers, feasts, charity, and family gatherings.
23 MarchMondayShaheedi Diwas / Martyrdom Day of Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and SukhdevHonours Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev, hanged by the British at Lahore in 1931.
26 MarchThursdayRam NavmiMarks the birth of Rama, the seventh avatar of Vishnu, celebrated with readings of the Ramayana.
31 MarchTuesdayMahavir JayantiMarks the birth of Mahavira, the twenty-fourth and last Tirthankara of Jainism.
14 AprilTuesdayDr. B. R. Ambedkar Jayanti / VaisakhiThe birth of B. R. Ambedkar, falling with Vaisakhi, the spring harvest festival and Sikh new year.
19 AprilSundayParshuram Jayanti / Akshay TritiyaThe appearance of Parashurama, falling with Akshaya Tritiya, held to be an auspicious day for new beginnings.
27 MayWednesdayId-ul-Zuha (Bakrid)The festival of sacrifice, commemorating Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, marked by the sharing of meat.
17 JuneWednesdayMaharana Pratap JayantiMarks the birth of Maharana Pratap of Mewar, remembered for refusing to submit to Mughal rule.
29 JuneMondaySant Kabir JayantiMarks the birth of Kabir, the mystic poet whose verses reject both ritualism and sectarian division.
31 JulyFridayShaheed Udham Singh Martyrdom DayHonours Udham Singh, executed in 1940 for the killing that avenged the Jallianwala Bagh massacre.
15 AugustSaturdayIndependence DayMarks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947, observed with flag hoisting and national ceremonies.
28 AugustFridayRaksha BandhanSisters tie a protective thread on their brothers' wrists in exchange for a promise of protection.
4 SeptemberFridayJanmashtamiMarks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.
23 SeptemberWednesdayShaheed Divas / Haryana War Heroes's Martyrdom DayHonours the soldiers of Haryana who died in service.
2 OctoberFridayMahatma Gandhi JayantiBirth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement and advocate of non-violent resistance.
11 OctoberSundayMaharaja Agrasen JayantiMarks the birth of Maharaja Agrasen, the legendary king held to have founded Agroha and the Agrawal community.
20 OctoberTuesdayDussehraVijaya Dashami, marking Rama's victory over Ravana and Durga's over Mahishasura; effigies are burnt across northern India.
26 OctoberMondayMaharishi Valmiki JayantiMarks the birth of Valmiki, author of the Ramayana.
1 NovemberSundayHaryana DayMarks the formation of Haryana as a separate state in 1966.
8 NovemberSundayDiwaliThe festival of lights, marking the triumph of light over darkness and Rama's return to Ayodhya.
9 NovemberMondayVishvakarma DayWorship of Vishwakarma, the divine architect, on which tools and machinery are honoured.
24 NovemberTuesdayGuru Nanak Dev JayantiMarks the birth of Guru Nanak, founder of Sikhism, observed with processions and scripture readings.
25 DecemberFridayChristmas DayCelebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.

Restricted holidays (14)

DateDayHoliday
12 FebruaryThursdayGuru Brahmanand Jayanti / Maharishi Dayanand Saraswati JayantiMarks the birth of Guru Brahmanand, the Haryana poet-saint, alongside that of Dayanand Saraswati, founder of the Arya Samaj.
1 AprilWednesdayAnnual Closing of Bank AccountThe annual closing of bank accounts on 1 April. Banks are shut; government offices stay open.
3 AprilFridayGood FridayCommemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, observed by Christians with fasting, prayer and church services.
1 MayFridayBuddha PurnimaMarks the birth, enlightenment and passing of Gautama Buddha, all traditionally held to have fallen on the same full-moon day.
25 MayMondayMaharishi Kashyap JayantiHonours the sage Kashyap, revered as an ancestor in Vedic tradition and by the Kashyap community.
18 JuneThursdayGuru Arjan Dev's Martyrdom DayMarks the martyrdom of the fifth Sikh Guru, tortured to death at Lahore in 1606.
26 JuneFridayMuharramThe first month of the Islamic year; the tenth day, Ashura, is kept in mourning for the martyrdom of Imam Husayn at Karbala.
15 AugustSaturdayHariyali TeejThe monsoon festival on which women swing, wear green and fast for marital well-being.
26 AugustWednesdayMilad-un-Nabi or Id-e-MiladMarks the birth of the Prophet Muhammad, observed with processions, readings and charity.
29 OctoberThursdayKarva ChauthA day-long fast kept by married women for their husbands' well-being, broken on sighting the moon.
9 NovemberMondayGoverdhan PujaMarks Krishna lifting Mount Govardhan to shelter the villagers from Indra's storm, kept the day after Diwali.
15 NovemberSundayChhath PujaFour days of offerings to the setting and rising sun, the principal festival of Bihar and eastern Uttar Pradesh.
14 DecemberMondayGuru Teg Bahadur's Martyrdom DayMarks the execution of the ninth Sikh Guru in 1675 for defending the right to religious freedom.
26 DecemberSaturdayShaheed Udham Singh's JayantiMarks the birth of Udham Singh, who avenged the Jallianwala Bagh massacre.
