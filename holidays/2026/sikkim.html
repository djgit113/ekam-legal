In 2026, Sikkim has 41 gazetted holidays — days when state government offices are closed. The state's list has no restricted holidays.

All 41 holidays in 2026

January 4

  1. Jan1

    New Year's Day

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 1 January

    Marks the first day of the year on the Gregorian calendar, celebrated globally with festivities and new resolutions.

  2. Jan15

    Maghe Sankranti

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 15 January

    Marks the sun's passage into Capricorn, kept in Nepal and Sikkim with ritual bathing and sweet foods.

  3. Jan19

    Sonam Lochhar

    Gazetted

    Monday · 19 January

    The new year of the Tamang community, kept in Sikkim and Nepal.

  4. Jan26

    Republic Day

    Gazetted

    Monday · 26 January

    Marks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.

February 2

  1. Feb18

    Losar

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 18 February

    The Tibetan and Bhutia new year, kept in Sikkim with monastery dances and family gatherings.

  2. Feb26

    Akhil Sikkim Khas Chettri Bahun Kalyan Sangh's Foundation Day

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 26 February

    The foundation day of the welfare association of Sikkim's Khas, Chettri and Bahun communities.

March 4

  1. Mar3

    Bhumchu

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 3 March

    The Sikkimese festival at Tashiding monastery, at which the water in a sacred pot is read as an omen for the year ahead.

  2. Mar3

    Holi

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 3 March

    The festival of colours, marking the arrival of spring and the triumph of good over evil.

  3. Mar21

    Id-Ul-Fitr

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 21 March

    The Islamic festival marking the end of Ramadan, celebrated with communal prayers, feasts, charity, and family gatherings.

  4. Mar27

    Ramnawami (Chaite Dasain)

    Gazetted

    Friday · 27 March

    Marks the birth of Rama, kept in the Nepali tradition as Chaite Dasain, the spring Dasain.

April 2

  1. Apr3

    Good Friday

    Gazetted

    Friday · 3 April

    Commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, observed by Christians with fasting, prayer and church services.

  2. Apr14

    Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Jayanti

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 14 April

    Marks the birth of B. R. Ambedkar, principal architect of the Constitution of India.

May 3

  1. May1

    Shyadar Pidar

    Gazetted

    Friday · 1 May

    A Sikkimese observance kept on the first of May.

  2. May16

    State Day

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 16 May

    Marks the day the state was formed.

  3. May31

    Saga Dawa

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 31 May

    The Buddhist month marking the Buddha's birth, enlightenment and passing, its full moon the holiest day of the year.

July 3

  1. Jul13

    Bhanu Jayanti

    Gazetted

    Monday · 13 July

    Marks the birth of Bhanubhakta Acharya, the poet who first translated the Ramayana into Nepali.

  2. Jul18

    Drukpa Tshe-zi

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 18 July

    Marks the Buddha's first sermon at Sarnath, kept on the fourth day of the sixth Tibetan month.

  3. Jul24

    Guru Rimpoche's Thungkar Tshechu

    Gazetted

    Friday · 24 July

    Marks the birth of Padmasambhava, Guru Rinpoche, who brought Buddhism to the Himalayas.

August 4

  1. Aug8

    Tendong Lho Rum Faat

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 8 August

    The Lepcha festival thanking Mount Tendong for sheltering the people from a great flood.

  2. Aug15

    Independence Day

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 15 August

    Marks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947, observed with flag hoisting and national ceremonies.

  3. Aug20

    Nepali Bhasa Manyata Diwas

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 20 August

    Marks the recognition of Nepali in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution in 1992.

  4. Aug28

    Pang Lhabsol

    Gazetted

    Friday · 28 August

    The Sikkimese festival honouring Kanchenjunga as guardian deity, marking the blood-brotherhood treaty between the Lepcha and Bhutia.

September 3

  1. Sep4

    Janmashtami

    Gazetted

    Friday · 4 September

    Marks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.

  2. Sep14

    Teej (Haritalika)

    Gazetted

    Monday · 14 September

    A waterless fast kept by women in honour of Parvati's union with Shiva.

  3. Sep25

    Indrajatra

    Gazetted

    Friday · 25 September

    The Newar festival of Kathmandu origin, kept in Sikkim with masked dances in honour of Indra.

October 3

  1. Oct2

    Gandhi Jayanti

    Gazetted

    Friday · 2 October

    Birth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement and advocate of non-violent resistance.

  2. Oct5

    Birthday of Late Nar Bahadur Bhandari, Former Chief Minister of Sikkim

    Gazetted

    Monday · 5 October

    Marks the birth of Nar Bahadur Bhandari, three times Chief Minister of Sikkim.

  3. Oct19–23

    Durga Puja (Dasain)

    Gazetted

    Monday – Friday · 19–23 October

    Dasain, the principal festival of the Nepali-speaking communities, marking Durga's victory over Mahishasura.

November 3

  1. Nov1

    Lhabab Duechen

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 1 November

    Marks the Buddha's descent from the heaven of the thirty-three gods after teaching his mother.

  2. Nov9–11

    Laxmi Puja (Deepawali)

    Gazetted

    Monday – Wednesday · 9–11 November

    The worship of Lakshmi at Diwali, for prosperity in the year ahead.

  3. Nov30

    Sakewa

    Gazetted

    Monday · 30 November

    The Rai community's earth-worship festival in Sikkim, offering the first fruits and praying for a good harvest.

December 10

  1. Dec7

    Kagyed Dance

    Gazetted

    Monday · 7 December

    The masked dance held in Sikkim's monasteries at the year's end to burn away the evils of the past year.

  2. Dec9–14

    Losoong / Namsoong

    Gazetted

    Wednesday – Monday · 9–14 December

    The Sikkimese harvest new year, kept by the Bhutia as Losoong and the Lepcha as Namsoong.

  3. Dec15

    Chasok Tongnam

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 15 December

    The Limbu harvest thanksgiving of Sikkim, at which the first grain is offered before it is eaten.

  4. Dec15

    Nyengpa Guzom

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 15 December

    A Sikkimese Buddhist observance kept in the twelfth month, held to be an inauspicious day for new work.

  5. Dec16

    Nyengpa Guzom

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 16 December

    A Sikkimese Buddhist observance kept in the twelfth month, held to be an inauspicious day for new work.

  6. Dec23

    Puhgal Parim

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 23 December

    A Sikkimese observance kept in the twelfth month of the Tibetan calendar.

  7. Dec23

    Teyongsi Sirijunga Sawan Tongnam

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 23 December

    The Limbu festival honouring Teyongsi Sirijunga, who revived the Limbu script.

  8. Dec25

    Christmas

    Gazetted

    Friday · 25 December

    Celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.

  9. Dec28

    Barahimijong

    Gazetted

    Monday · 28 December

    A festival of the Limbu community of Sikkim, observed as a state holiday.

  10. Dec30

    Tamu Lochar

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 30 December

    The new year of the Gurung community, kept in Sikkim and Nepal.
