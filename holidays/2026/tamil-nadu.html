In 2026, Tamil Nadu has 23 gazetted holidays and 1 restricted holiday. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

All 24 holidays in 2026

January 5

  1. Jan1

    New Year's Day

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 1 January

    Marks the first day of the year on the Gregorian calendar, celebrated globally with festivities and new resolutions.

  2. Jan15

    Pongal

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 15 January

    The Tamil harvest festival, thanking the sun for the crop, named after the rice dish boiled over until it spills.

  3. Jan16

    Thiruvalluvar Day

    Gazetted

    Friday · 16 January

    Honours Thiruvalluvar, author of the Thirukkural, kept as part of the Pongal season in Tamil Nadu.

  4. Jan17

    Uzhavar Thirunal

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 17 January

    The farmers' day of the Pongal season in Tamil Nadu, honouring those who grow the crop.

  5. Jan26

    Republic Day

    Gazetted

    Monday · 26 January

    Marks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.

February 1

  1. Feb1

    Thai Poosam

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 1 February

    The Tamil festival honouring Murugan, marked by pilgrimage and the carrying of kavadi.

March 3

  1. Mar19

    Telugu New Year's Day

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 19 March

    Ugadi, the new year of the Telugu and Kannada calendars.

  2. Mar21

    Ramzan (Idu'l Fitr)

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 21 March

    Marks the end of Ramadan's month of fasting, celebrated with congregational prayer and family meals.

  3. Mar31

    Mahaveer Jayanthi

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 31 March

    Marks the birth of Mahavira, the twenty-fourth and last Tirthankara of Jainism.

April 3

  1. Apr1

    Annual Closing of Bank Accounts

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 1 April

    The annual closing of bank accounts on 1 April. Banks are shut; government offices stay open.

  2. Apr3

    Good Friday

    Gazetted

    Friday · 3 April

    Commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, observed by Christians with fasting, prayer and church services.

  3. Apr14

    Tamil New Year's Day / Dr. B. R. Ambedkar's Birthday

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 14 April

    Puthandu, the Tamil new year, falling with the birth anniversary of B. R. Ambedkar.

May 2

  1. May1

    May Day

    Gazetted

    Friday · 1 May

    International Workers' Day, marking the labour movement's campaign for the eight-hour working day.

  2. May28

    Bakrid (Idul Azha)

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 28 May

    The festival of sacrifice, commemorating Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, marked by the sharing of meat.

June 1

  1. Jun26

    Muharram (Yaom-E-Shahadath)

    Gazetted

    Friday · 26 June

    The day of martyrdom, kept in mourning for Imam Husayn at Karbala.

August 2

  1. Aug15

    Independence Day

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 15 August

    Marks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947, observed with flag hoisting and national ceremonies.

  2. Aug26

    Milad-un-Nabi

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 26 August

    Marks the birth of the Prophet Muhammad, observed with processions, readings and charity.

September 2

  1. Sep4

    Krishna Jayanthi

    Gazetted

    Friday · 4 September

    Marks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.

  2. Sep14

    Vinayakar Chathurthi

    Gazetted

    Monday · 14 September

    Marks the birth of Ganesha, remover of obstacles, whose images are worshipped and then immersed.

October 3

  1. Oct2

    Gandhi Jayanthi

    Gazetted

    Friday · 2 October

    Birth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement.

  2. Oct19

    Ayutha Pooja

    Gazetted

    Monday · 19 October

    The worship of tools, books and machinery on the ninth day of Navratri, kept across Tamil Nadu.

  3. Oct20

    Vijaya Dasami

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 20 October

    The tenth day of Navratri, marking the victory of good over evil and the close of the festival.

November 1

  1. Nov8

    Deepavali

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 8 November

    The festival of lights, marking the triumph of light over darkness and Rama's return to Ayodhya.

December 1

  1. Dec25

    Christmas

    Gazetted

    Friday · 25 December

    Celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.
