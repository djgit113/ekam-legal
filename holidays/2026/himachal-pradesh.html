Himachal Pradesh Holiday List 2026

Gazetted and restricted holidays, with dates and weekdays

In 2026, Himachal Pradesh has 24 gazetted holidays and 12 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

Gazetted holidays (24)

DateDayHoliday
25 JanuarySundayStatehood DayMarks the day Arunachal Pradesh became a full state of the Indian Union in 1987.
26 JanuaryMondayRepublic DayMarks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.
1 FebruarySundayGuru Ravidas's BirthdayMarks the birth of Ravidas, the fifteenth-century poet-saint of the Bhakti movement.
15 FebruarySundayMaha ShivratriThe great night of Shiva, kept with fasting and night-long worship.
4 MarchWednesdayHoliThe festival of colours, marking the arrival of spring and the triumph of good over evil.
21 MarchSaturdayId-ul-FitrThe Islamic festival marking the end of Ramadan, celebrated with communal prayers, feasts, charity, and family gatherings.
26 MarchThursdayRam NavmiMarks the birth of Rama, the seventh avatar of Vishnu, celebrated with readings of the Ramayana.
3 AprilFridayGood FridayCommemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, observed by Christians with fasting, prayer and church services.
14 AprilTuesdayDr. B. R. Ambedkar's BirthdayMarks the birth of B. R. Ambedkar, principal architect of the Constitution of India and campaigner against caste discrimination.
15 AprilWednesdayHimachal DayMarks the formation of Himachal Pradesh as a centrally administered province in 1948.
19 AprilSundayBhagvan Shree Parshuram JayantiMarks the appearance of Parashurama, the sixth avatar of Vishnu.
1 MayFridayBuddha PurnimaMarks the birth, enlightenment and passing of Gautama Buddha, all traditionally held to have fallen on the same full-moon day.
27 MayWednesdayId-ul-Zuha (Bakrid)The festival of sacrifice, commemorating Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, marked by the sharing of meat.
17 JuneWednesdayMaharana Pratap JayantiMarks the birth of Maharana Pratap of Mewar, remembered for refusing to submit to Mughal rule.
26 JuneFridayMuharramThe first month of the Islamic year; the tenth day, Ashura, is kept in mourning for the martyrdom of Imam Husayn at Karbala.
29 JuneMondaySant Guru Kabir Jayanti (Prakat Diwas)Marks the birth of Kabir, the mystic poet whose verses reject both ritualism and sectarian division.
15 AugustSaturdayIndependence DayMarks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947, observed with flag hoisting and national ceremonies.
4 SeptemberFridayJanmashtamiMarks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.
2 OctoberFridayMahatma Gandhi's BirthdayBirth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement and advocate of non-violent resistance.
20 OctoberTuesdayDussehraVijaya Dashami, marking Rama's victory over Ravana and Durga's over Mahishasura; effigies are burnt across northern India.
26 OctoberMondayMaharishi Valmiki's BirthdayMarks the birth of Valmiki, author of the Ramayana.
8 NovemberSundayDiwali (Deepavali)The festival of lights, marking the triumph of light over darkness and Rama's return to Ayodhya.
24 NovemberTuesdayGuru Nanak's BirthdayMarks the birth of Guru Nanak, the founder of Sikhism, observed with processions and scripture readings.
25 DecemberFridayChristmas DayCelebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.

Restricted holidays (12)

DateDayHoliday
1 JanuaryThursdayNew Year's DayMarks the first day of the year on the Gregorian calendar, celebrated globally with festivities and new resolutions.
14 JanuaryWednesdayMakar SankrantiMarks the sun's passage into Capricorn and the start of longer days, celebrated with kite flying, bonfires and sesame sweets.
23 JanuaryFridaySri Panchami, Basant PanchamiMarks the arrival of spring and the worship of Saraswati, goddess of learning.
12 FebruaryThursdayBirthday of Swami Dayanand SaraswatiMarks the birth of the founder of the Arya Samaj, a reformer who called for a return to the Vedas.
31 MarchTuesdayMahavir JayantiMarks the birth of Mahavira, the twenty-fourth and last Tirthankara of Jainism.
5 AprilSundayEaster SundayCelebrates the resurrection of Jesus Christ on the third day after his crucifixion, the central festival of the Christian year.
9 MaySaturdayBirthday of Guru Rabindranath TagoreMarks the birth of Rabindranath Tagore, poet and composer, the first non-European to win the Nobel Prize in Literature.
14 SeptemberMondayGanesh Chaturthi / Vinayaka ChaturthiMarks the birth of Ganesha, remover of obstacles, whose images are worshipped and then immersed.
19 OctoberMondayDussehra (Mahashtami)The eighth day of the Navratri festival, among its most sacred.
9 NovemberMondayGovardhan PujaMarks Krishna lifting Mount Govardhan to shelter the villagers from Indra's storm, kept the day after Diwali.
24 NovemberTuesdayGuru Teg Bahadur's Martyrdom DayMarks the execution of the ninth Sikh Guru in 1675 for defending the right to religious freedom.
24 DecemberThursdayChristmas EveThe evening before Christmas Day, marked with midnight services and family celebrations.

Gazetted holidays on a Monday or Friday

These sit next to a weekend — a long weekend for anyone who has the Saturday off.

  • Monday, 26 January — Republic Day
  • Friday, 3 April — Good Friday
  • Friday, 1 May — Buddha Purnima
  • Friday, 26 June — Muharram
  • Monday, 29 June — Sant Guru Kabir Jayanti (Prakat Diwas)
  • Friday, 4 September — Janmashtami
  • Friday, 2 October — Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday
  • Monday, 26 October — Maharishi Valmiki's Birthday
  • Friday, 25 December — Christmas Day
