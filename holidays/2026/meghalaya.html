Meghalaya Holiday List 2026

Gazetted and restricted holidays, with dates and weekdays

In 2026, Meghalaya has 23 gazetted holidays and 10 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

See these holidays on a calendar, plan your leave around them and get reminders — with Ekam, a free Android app.
