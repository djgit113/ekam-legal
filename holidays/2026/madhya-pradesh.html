Madhya Pradesh Holiday List 2026

Every gazetted and restricted holiday in Madhya Pradesh for 2026, from the state's own published list — with dates, weekdays and the long weekends.

In 2026, Madhya Pradesh has 29 gazetted holidays and 75 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

All 104 holidays in 2026

January 10

  1. Jan1

    New Year Day

    Restricted

    Thursday · 1 January

    Marks the first day of the year on the Gregorian calendar, celebrated globally with festivities and new resolutions.

  2. Jan3

    Hazrat Ali's Birthday

    Restricted

    Saturday · 3 January

    Marks the birth of Ali ibn Abi Talib, cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet Muhammad.

  3. Jan6

    Maharishi Guru Gokuldas Ji Maharaj's Birthday

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 6 January

    Marks the birth of Guru Gokuldas Ji Maharaj, revered in central India.

  4. Jan14

    Makar Sankranti / Pongal

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 14 January

  5. Jan21

    Hemu Kalani's Martyrdom Day

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 21 January

    Honours Hemu Kalani, the Sindhi revolutionary hanged by the British in 1943 at the age of nineteen.

  6. Jan23

    Basant Panchami

    Restricted

    Friday · 23 January

    Marks the arrival of spring and the worship of Saraswati, goddess of learning.

  7. Jan24

    Dev Narayan Jayanti

    Restricted

    Saturday · 24 January

    Marks the birth of Devnarayan, the folk deity revered as an incarnation of Vishnu in Rajasthan.

  8. Jan25

    Narmada Jayanti

    Restricted

    Sunday · 25 January

    Marks the appearance of the river Narmada, worshipped as a goddess across central India.

  9. Jan26

    Republic Day

    Gazetted

    Monday · 26 January

    Marks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.

  10. Jan31

    Swami Ramcharan Ji Maharaj's Birthday

    Restricted

    Saturday · 31 January

    Marks the birth of Swami Ramcharan, founder of the Ramsnehi sect of Rajasthan.

  7. Feb19
