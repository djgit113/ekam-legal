In 2026, Gujarat has 27 gazetted holidays and 50 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

All 77 holidays in 2026

January 5

  1. Jan1

    Christian New Year Day

    Restricted

    Thursday · 1 January

    The first day of the year on the Gregorian calendar.

  2. Jan14

    Makar Sankranti

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 14 January

    Marks the sun's passage into Capricorn and the start of longer days, celebrated with kite flying, bonfires and sesame sweets.

  3. Jan15

    Vassi Uttarayan

    Restricted

    Thursday · 15 January

    The second day of Uttarayan in Gujarat, when kite flying continues.

  4. Jan26

    Republic Day

    Gazetted

    Monday · 26 January

    Marks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.

  5. Jan31

    Vishvakarma Jayanti

    Restricted

    Saturday · 31 January

    Worship of Vishwakarma, the divine architect, on which tools and machinery are honoured.
