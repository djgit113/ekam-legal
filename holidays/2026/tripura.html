Tripura Holiday List 2026

Gazetted and restricted holidays, with dates and weekdays

In 2026, Tripura has 33 gazetted holidays and 14 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.
