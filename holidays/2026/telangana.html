In 2026, Telangana has 27 gazetted holidays and 26 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

All 53 holidays in 2026

January 8

  1. Jan1

    New Year Day

    Restricted

    Thursday · 1 January

    Marks the first day of the year on the Gregorian calendar, celebrated globally with festivities and new resolutions.

  2. Jan3

    Birthday of Hazrath Ali (R.A.)

    Restricted

    Saturday · 3 January

    Marks the birth of Ali ibn Abi Talib, cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet Muhammad.

  3. Jan14

    Bhogi

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 14 January

    The first day of Pongal, on which old possessions are burnt to mark a fresh start.

  4. Jan15

    Sankranti / Pongal

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 15 January

    Marks the sun's passage into Capricorn, kept as the harvest thanksgiving of Pongal.

  5. Jan16

    Kanumu

    Restricted

    Friday · 16 January

    The third day of Sankranti, on which cattle are honoured for their part in the harvest.

  6. Jan17

    Shab-e-Meraj

    Restricted

    Saturday · 17 January

    Marks the Prophet Muhammad's night journey and ascension through the heavens.

  7. Jan23

    Sri Panchami

    Restricted

    Friday · 23 January

    Marks the arrival of spring and the worship of Saraswati, goddess of learning.

  8. Jan26

    Republic Day

    Gazetted

    Monday · 26 January

    Marks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.

February 2

  1. Feb4

    Shab-e-Barat

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 4 February

    The night of records, kept with prayer and remembrance of the dead in the month before Ramadan.

  2. Feb15

    Maha Shivaratri

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 15 February

    The great night of Shiva, kept with fasting and night-long worship.

March 9

  1. Mar3

    Holi

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 3 March

    The festival of colours, marking the arrival of spring and the triumph of good over evil.

  2. Mar10

    Shahadat Hzt Ali (R.A.)

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 10 March

    Marks the martyrdom of Ali ibn Abi Talib, the fourth caliph.

  3. Mar13

    Jumuatul Wada

    Restricted

    Friday · 13 March

    The last Friday of Ramadan, drawing the largest congregational prayers of the year.

  4. Mar17

    Shab-e-Qader

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 17 March

    The Night of Power in the last ten days of Ramadan, held to be the night the Quran was first revealed.

  5. Mar19

    Ugadi

    Gazetted

    Thursday · 19 March

    The new year of the Deccan, marked with a preparation combining six tastes to represent the year's mixture of experiences.

  6. Mar21

    Eidul Fitr (Ramzan)

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 21 March

    Marks the end of Ramadan's month of fasting, celebrated with congregational prayer and family meals.

  7. Mar22

    Following Day of Ramzan

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 22 March

    The second day of Eid-ul-Fitr, kept for visiting family.

  8. Mar27

    Sri Rama Navami

    Gazetted

    Friday · 27 March

    Marks the birth of Rama, the seventh avatar of Vishnu, celebrated with readings of the Ramayana.

  9. Mar31

    Mahaveer Jayanthi

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 31 March

    Marks the birth of Mahavira, the twenty-fourth and last Tirthankara of Jainism.

April 5

  1. Apr3

    Good Friday

    Gazetted

    Friday · 3 April

    Commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, observed by Christians with fasting, prayer and church services.

  2. Apr5

    Babu Jagjivan Ram's Birthday

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 5 April

    Marks the birth of Jagjivan Ram, freedom fighter, Deputy Prime Minister and campaigner for the depressed classes.

  3. Apr14

    Dr. B. R. Ambedkar's Birthday

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 14 April

    Marks the birth of B. R. Ambedkar, principal architect of the Constitution of India and campaigner against caste discrimination.

  4. Apr14

    Tamil New Year's Day

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 14 April

    Puthandu, the first day of the Tamil month of Chithirai.

  5. Apr20

    Basava Jayanthi

    Restricted

    Monday · 20 April

    Marks the birth of Basavanna, the twelfth-century reformer and founder of the Lingayat tradition.

May 2

  1. May1

    Buddha Purnima

    Restricted

    Friday · 1 May

    Marks the birth, enlightenment and passing of Gautama Buddha, all traditionally held to have fallen on the same full-moon day.

  2. May27

    Eidul Azha (Bakrid)

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 27 May

    The festival of sacrifice, commemorating Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, marked by the sharing of meat.

June 3

  1. Jun4

    Eid-e-Ghadeer

    Restricted

    Thursday · 4 June

    Marks the Prophet Muhammad's declaration at Ghadir Khumm, observed chiefly by Shia Muslims.

  2. Jun25

    9th Moharram

    Restricted

    Thursday · 25 June

    The ninth day of Muharram, the eve of Ashura, kept in mourning for the martyrdom of Imam Husayn.

  3. Jun26

    Shahadat Imam Hussain (R.A.) 10th Moharam

    Gazetted

    Friday · 26 June

    Ashura, the tenth of Muharram, marking the martyrdom of Imam Husayn at Karbala.

July 1

  1. Jul16

    Ratha Yathra

    Restricted

    Thursday · 16 July

    The chariot festival of Jagannath, in which the deities are drawn through the streets in great wooden chariots.

August 7

  1. Aug4

    Arbayeen

    Restricted

    Tuesday · 4 August

    The fortieth day after Ashura, closing the mourning period for Imam Husayn.

  2. Aug10

    Bonalu

    Gazetted

    Monday · 10 August

    The Telangana festival honouring the goddess Mahankali, in which cooked rice is carried to the temple in decorated pots.

  3. Aug15

    Independence Day

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 15 August

    Marks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947, observed with flag hoisting and national ceremonies.

  4. Aug15

    Parsi New Year's Day

    Restricted

    Saturday · 15 August

    Navroz, the Parsi new year, marking renewal and the start of the Zoroastrian year.

  5. Aug21

    Varalakshmi Vratham

    Restricted

    Friday · 21 August

    A Friday fast kept by married women in southern India for the family's prosperity and well-being.

  6. Aug26

    Eid Miladun Nabi

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 26 August

    Marks the birth of the Prophet Muhammad, observed with processions, readings and charity.

  7. Aug28

    Sravana Purnima / Rakhi Purnima

    Restricted

    Friday · 28 August

    The Shravana full moon, on which sisters tie a protective thread on their brothers' wrists.

September 3

  1. Sep4

    Sri Krishna Astami

    Gazetted

    Friday · 4 September

    Marks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.

  2. Sep14

    Vinayaka Chavithi

    Gazetted

    Monday · 14 September

    The Andhra observance of Ganesha's birth, with clay images worshipped at home and in pandals.

  3. Sep23

    Yaz Dahum Shareef

    Restricted

    Wednesday · 23 September

    The eleventh-day observance honouring Abdul Qadir Jilani, founder of the Qadiriyya order.

October 6

  1. Oct2

    Mahatma Gandhi Jayanthi

    Gazetted

    Friday · 2 October

    Birth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement and advocate of non-violent resistance.

  2. Oct18

    Saddula Bathukamma

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 18 October

    The closing day of Telangana's Bathukamma festival, when the flower stacks are set afloat on water.

  3. Oct19

    Maharnavami

    Restricted

    Monday · 19 October

    The ninth day of Navratri, the last before Vijaya Dashami.

  4. Oct20

    Vijaya Dasami / Dushera

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 20 October

    The tenth day, marking Rama's victory over Ravana and Durga's over Mahishasura.

  5. Oct21

    Following Day of Vijaya Dasami

    Gazetted

    Wednesday · 21 October

    An additional day after Vijaya Dashami, kept in Telangana as the festival closes.

  6. Oct26

    Birthday of Hzt. Syed Mohammed Juvanpuri Mahdi Ma'ud (A.S.)

    Restricted

    Monday · 26 October

    Marks the birth of Syed Muhammad Jaunpuri, revered as the Mahdi by the Mahdavia community.

November 3

  1. Nov8

    Deepavali

    Gazetted

    Sunday · 8 November

    The festival of lights, marking the triumph of light over darkness and Rama's return to Ayodhya.

  2. Nov8

    Naraka Chaturdhi

    Restricted

    Sunday · 8 November

    Chhoti Diwali, marking Krishna's defeat of the demon Narakasura on the day before Diwali.

  3. Nov24

    Kartika Purnima / Guru Nanak's Jayanthi

    Gazetted

    Tuesday · 24 November

    The Kartik full moon, on which lamps are floated on rivers, falling with the birth of Guru Nanak.

December 4

  1. Dec24

    Christmas Eve

    Restricted

    Thursday · 24 December

    The evening before Christmas Day, marked with midnight services and family celebrations.

  2. Dec25

    Christmas

    Gazetted

    Friday · 25 December

    Celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.

  3. Dec26

    Birthday of Hazrath Ali

    Restricted

    Saturday · 26 December

    Marks the birth of Ali ibn Abi Talib, cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet Muhammad.

  4. Dec26

    Following Day of Christmas (Boxing Day)

    Gazetted

    Saturday · 26 December

    The day after Christmas, kept as a public holiday.
