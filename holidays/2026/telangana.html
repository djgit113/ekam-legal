Telangana Holiday List 2026

Gazetted and restricted holidays, with dates and weekdays

In 2026, Telangana has 27 gazetted holidays and 26 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

Gazetted holidays (27)

DateDayHoliday
14 JanuaryWednesdayBhogiThe first day of Pongal, on which old possessions are burnt to mark a fresh start.

18 OctoberSundaySaddula BathukammaThe closing day of Telangana's Bathukamma festival, when the flower stacks are set afloat on water.
20 OctoberTuesdayVijaya Dasami / DusheraThe tenth day, marking Rama's victory over Ravana and Durga's over Mahishasura.

25 DecemberFridayChristmasCelebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.
26 DecemberSaturdayFollowing Day of Christmas (Boxing Day)The day after Christmas, kept as a public holiday.

Gazetted holidays on a Monday or Friday

  • Friday, 27 March — Sri Rama Navami

  • Friday, 26 June — Shahadat Imam Hussain (R.A.) 10th Moharam
  • Monday, 10 August — Bonalu
  • Friday, 4 September — Sri Krishna Astami
  • Monday, 14 September — Vinayaka Chavithi
  • Friday, 2 October — Mahatma Gandhi Jayanthi
  • Friday, 25 December — Christmas
