Punjab Holiday List 2026

Gazetted and restricted holidays, with dates and weekdays

In 2026, Punjab has 31 gazetted holidays and 27 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

Gazetted holidays (31)

DateDayHoliday
26 JanuaryMondayRepublic DayMarks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.

26 MarchThursdayRam NavamiMarks the birth of Rama, the seventh avatar of Vishnu, celebrated with readings of the Ramayana.

1 MayFridayMay DayInternational Workers' Day, marking the labour movement's campaign for the eight-hour working day.
27 MayWednesdayEid-ul-Zuha (Bakrid)The festival of sacrifice, commemorating Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, marked by the sharing of meat.
18 JuneThursdayShaheedi Diwas of Sri Guru Arjan Dev JiMarks the martyrdom of the fifth Sikh Guru, tortured to death at Lahore in 1606.
29 JuneMondayKabir JayantiMarks the birth of Kabir, the mystic poet whose verses reject both ritualism and sectarian division.
31 JulyFridayShaheedi Dihara of Shaheed Udham Singh JiHonours Udham Singh, executed in 1940 for the killing that avenged the Jallianwala Bagh massacre.
15 AugustSaturdayIndependence DayMarks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947, observed with flag hoisting and national ceremonies.
4 SeptemberFridayJanam AshtamiMarks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.
2 OctoberFridayBirthday of Mahatma Gandhi JiBirth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement.
11 OctoberSundayMaharaja Agrasen JayantiMarks the birth of Maharaja Agrasen, the legendary king held to have founded Agroha and the Agrawal community.
20 OctoberTuesdayDussehraVijaya Dashami, marking Rama's victory over Ravana and Durga's over Mahishasura; effigies are burnt across northern India.
26 OctoberMondayBirthday of Maharishi Valmiki JiMarks the birth of Valmiki, author of the Ramayana.
8 NovemberSundayDiwaliThe festival of lights, marking the triumph of light over darkness and Rama's return to Ayodhya.
9 NovemberMondayVishwakarma DiwasWorship of Vishwakarma, the divine architect, on which tools and machinery are honoured.
16 NovemberMondayShaheedi Diwas of S. Kartar Singh Sarabha JiHonours Kartar Singh Sarabha of the Ghadar Party, hanged in 1915 at the age of nineteen.
24 NovemberTuesdayGurpurab Sahib of Sri Guru Nanak Dev JiMarks the birth of Guru Nanak, founder of Sikhism, observed with processions and scripture readings.
14 DecemberMondayShaheedi Diwas of Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur JiMarks the execution of the ninth Sikh Guru in 1675 for defending the right to religious freedom.
25 DecemberFridayChristmas DayCelebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.
28 DecemberMondayShaheedi Sabha, Sri Fatehgarh SahibThe gathering at Fatehgarh Sahib remembering the younger sons of Guru Gobind Singh, bricked alive in 1705.

27 OctoberTuesdayPrakash Gurpurab of Sri Guru Ramdas Sahib JiMarks the birth of Guru Ram Das, the fourth Sikh Guru, who founded Amritsar.
29 OctoberThursdayKarva ChauthA day-long fast kept by married women for their husbands' well-being, broken on sighting the moon.
1 NovemberSundayNava Punjab DiwasMarks the reorganisation of Punjab in 1966, from which the present state took its shape.
9 NovemberMondayGovardhan PujaMarks Krishna lifting Mount Govardhan to shelter the villagers from Indra's storm, kept the day after Diwali.
11 NovemberWednesdayGurgaddi Diwas of Sri Guru Granth Sahib JiMarks the day Guru Gobind Singh conferred the Guruship on the Guru Granth Sahib in 1708.
15 NovemberSundayChhath PujaFour days of offerings to the setting and rising sun, the principal festival of Bihar and eastern Uttar Pradesh.
20 NovemberFridayBirthday of Sant Namdev JiMarks the birth of Sant Namdev, the Marathi poet-saint of the Bhakti movement.
