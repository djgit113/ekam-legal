Kerala Holiday List 2026

Gazetted and restricted holidays, with dates and weekdays

In 2026, Kerala has 25 gazetted holidays and 4 restricted holidays. Gazetted holidays are days when state government offices are closed. Restricted holidays are optional days that employees can apply to take off.

See these holidays on a calendar, plan your leave around them and get reminders — with Ekam, a free Android app.

Gazetted holidays (25)

DateDayHoliday
2 JanuaryFridayMannam JayanthiMarks the birth of Mannathu Padmanabhan, founder of the Nair Service Society in Kerala.
26 JanuaryMondayRepublic DayMarks the day in 1950 the Constitution of India came into force, replacing the Government of India Act as the country's governing document.
15 FebruarySundayMahasivarathriThe great night of Shiva, kept with fasting and night-long worship.
20 MarchFridayId-ul-Fitr (Ramzan)Marks the end of Ramadan's month of fasting, celebrated with congregational prayer and family meals.
2 AprilThursdayMaundy ThursdayCommemorates the Last Supper on the Thursday before Easter.
3 AprilFridayGood FridayCommemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, observed by Christians with fasting, prayer and church services.
5 AprilSundayEasterCelebrates the resurrection of Jesus Christ, the central festival of the Christian year.
14 AprilTuesdayDr. B. R. Ambedkar JayanthiMarks the birth of B. R. Ambedkar, principal architect of the Constitution of India.
15 AprilWednesdayVishuThe Malayalam new year, on which the first sight of the year is arranged as the auspicious Vishukkani.
1 MayFridayMay DayInternational Workers' Day, marking the labour movement's campaign for the eight-hour working day.
27 MayWednesdayId-ul-Ad'ha (Bakrid)The festival of sacrifice, commemorating Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son, marked by the sharing of meat.
25 JuneThursdayMuharramThe first month of the Islamic year; the tenth day, Ashura, is kept in mourning for the martyrdom of Imam Husayn at Karbala.
12 AugustWednesdayKarkadaka VavuThe Kerala new-moon day of Karkadakam, on which offerings are made to ancestors on river banks and beaches.
15 AugustSaturdayIndependence DayMarks India's independence from British rule on 15 August 1947, observed with flag hoisting and national ceremonies.
25 AugustTuesdayFirst Onam / Milad-i-SherifAtham, the opening day of Kerala's Onam harvest festival, falling with the Prophet Muhammad's birth anniversary.
26 AugustWednesdayThiruvonamThe chief day of Onam, on which King Mahabali is held to visit his people.
27 AugustThursdayThird OnamThe third day of Kerala's Onam harvest festival.
28 AugustFridayFourth Onam / Sree Narayana Guru Jayanthi / Ayyankali JayanthiThe fourth day of Onam, falling with the birth anniversaries of the Kerala reformers Narayana Guru and Ayyankali.
4 SeptemberFridaySreekrishna JayanthiMarks the birth of Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, kept with midnight worship and fasting.
21 SeptemberMondaySree Narayana Guru Samadhi DayMarks the death of Narayana Guru, the Kerala reformer who preached one caste, one religion, one God.
2 OctoberFridayGandhi JayanthiBirth anniversary of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, leader of the Indian independence movement.
20 OctoberTuesdayMahanavamiThe ninth day of Navratri, the last before Vijaya Dashami.
21 OctoberWednesdayVijayadasamiThe tenth day, marking the victory of good over evil and the close of the Navratri festival.
8 NovemberSundayDeepavaliThe festival of lights, marking the triumph of light over darkness and Rama's return to Ayodhya.
25 DecemberFridayChristmasCelebrates the birth of Jesus Christ, marked with church services, family gatherings and gift-giving.

Restricted holidays (4)

DateDayHoliday
4 MarchWednesdayAyya Vaikunda Swami JayanthiMarks the birth of Ayya Vaikundar, the nineteenth-century reformer revered by the Nadar community of southern India.
1 AprilWednesdayAnnual Closing of Bank AccountsThe annual closing of bank accounts on 1 April. Banks are shut; government offices stay open.
28 AugustFridayAvani AvittomThe day on which Brahmin men replace the sacred thread, kept on the Shravana full moon.
17 SeptemberThursdayVishwakarma DayWorship of Vishwakarma, the divine architect, on which tools and machinery are honoured.

Gazetted holidays on a Monday or Friday

  • Friday, 3 April — Good Friday
  • Friday, 1 May — May Day

  • Friday, 4 September — Sreekrishna Jayanthi
  • Monday, 21 September — Sree Narayana Guru Samadhi Day
  • Friday, 2 October — Gandhi Jayanthi
  • Friday, 25 December — Christmas
