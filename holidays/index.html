Not a government website Ekam is an independent app by N!rvana Labs. It is not made by, affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by any government or government department. Holiday dates are taken from each state government's published holiday list (every source is on the holiday sources page) and checked carefully, but mistakes can happen — please check your state's own list before making important plans. Holidays that a notification lists without a date, such as some district or local holidays, are not included. Found a wrong date? Write to support.nirvanalabs@gmail.com.
