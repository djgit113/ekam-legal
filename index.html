Legal

Holiday calendar, diary, reminders and planner for Android.

Ekam keeps everything you write on your own device. It has no account and no server of its own, and nothing you write is ever sent anywhere. The app is free and shows adverts; those are the one part of it that uses the internet, and the Privacy Policy sets out exactly what the ad network sees — and what it never does.
