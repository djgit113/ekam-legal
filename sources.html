Holiday sources

Every holiday in Ekam comes from a list published by a state government. Below is each state's list: who issued it, its reference and date, and a link to it on that government's own website, so you can check any date yourself.

Ekam is not a government app

Ekam is an independent app made by N!rvana Labs. It is not made by, affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by any government or government department.

Holiday dates are taken from the holiday list published by each state government, listed on this page. We check them carefully, but mistakes can happen. Before you make important plans, please check your state's own list using the link below.

How to check a date

  • Open your state's link above. Most go straight to the list itself.
  • Where a link opens a department's website or a government orders register (Andhra Pradesh, Haryana, Karnataka, Maharashtra and Sikkim), search that site for the reference number shown beside it.
  • Holidays that a list gives without a date, such as some district or local holidays, are not in Ekam.
  • Found a date in Ekam that does not match your state's list? Please write to support.nirvanalabs@gmail.com.
