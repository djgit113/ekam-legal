Holiday sources

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Ekam is not a government app

Ekam is an independent app made by N!rvana Labs. It is not made by, linked to, or approved by any government or government department.

The holidays in Ekam are copied from each state's published holiday list. We check them carefully, but mistakes can happen. Before you make important plans, please check the notification from your state government.

Notes

  • Manipur: when Ekam's Manipur list was made, we could not find the state government's notification, so these holidays come from a published list. That list does not separate gazetted and restricted holidays, so Ekam shows all of them as gazetted. The Government of Manipur has since published its own list (linked above); please check your dates against it.
  • Holidays that a notification lists without a date (for example, some district or local holidays) are not in Ekam.
